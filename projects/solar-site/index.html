How a home solar system works

  1. Solar panels turn sunlight into direct current (DC) electricity.
  2. An inverter converts DC into alternating current (AC) for your home.
  3. Your home uses that power first.
  4. Extra power goes to the grid; under net metering your utility may credit it.
  5. At night or on cloudy days you draw from the grid or a battery.

Reasons people consider solar

  • Lower or more predictable electric bills, especially where rates are high.
  • Clean power with no emissions while the panels operate.
  • Panels typically carry 25-year performance warranties.
  • A battery can keep key circuits running during an outage.

Things to weigh first

  • Upfront cost is significant, and financing adds interest.
  • Output depends on sun, shading, roof angle and weather.
  • Savings depend on your utility's rates and export credit rules.
  • Roof condition matters; replacing a roof later means removing panels.
