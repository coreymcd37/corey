Core Recipes & Portion Guide

Batch-friendly recipes designed as the backbone of your week. Season generously with fresh herbs, citrus, and spices.

Batch Cooking • Fresh Proteins & Roasted Vegetables

Lemon-Herb Chicken (4 servings)

Use 2 lb chicken breast, 1 lemon, 2 minced garlic cloves, oregano, paprika, pepper, and 1 tbsp olive oil. Roast at 400°F until the thickest piece reaches 165°F. Rest, slice, and refrigerate portions needed for the next 3–4 days; freeze the rest.

Turkey and Bean Chili (4 servings)

Brown 1.5 lb lean ground turkey with 1 chopped onion and peppers. Add 1 can drained beans, 1 can crushed tomatoes, cumin, and paprika. Simmer for 20–25 minutes. Serve alongside a fresh green salad.

Tofu-Edamame Stir-Fry (2–3 servings)

Cube a 14–16 oz block of calcium-set tofu. Brown in olive oil, then toss with broccoli florets, bell peppers, and 1 cup shelled edamame. Season with fresh ginger, garlic, and reduced-sodium soy sauce over brown rice.

Lentil-Tomato Pasta Sauce (4 servings)

Simmer cooked or canned lentils with crushed tomatoes, onions, garlic, and oregano. Toss with whole-wheat pasta and fresh broccoli.
